App competitor scorecard

Put your iOS app next to its App Store rivals: rating, number of ratings, monthly downloads and monthly revenue, with the change since last month. Paste one competitor comparison response below. Nothing leaves your browser.

Where to get the JSON

The input is the comparison response from PeekPanda, an iOS competitor catalogue that keeps dated evidence and nightly download and revenue estimates for each app. Ask for your app first, then up to nine rivals:

curl --get https://api.peekpanda.com/v1/competitors/compare \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $PEEKPANDA_TOKEN" \
  -H 'x-genviral-tenancy: personal' \
  --data-urlencode 'storeIds=1000000001,1000000002,1000000003'

Over MCP this is the search_apps tool with action compare. You need a PeekPanda account and an API key; the compare competitors reference lists the fields and errors.

How to read it

  • Downloads and revenue are worldwide monthly estimate ranges, not numbers reported by Apple or the developer. The bar is the mid estimate; the thin line spans low to high.
  • MoM compares this month's mid estimate with the one a month earlier. It is blank when either month is missing or below the floor.
  • "unknown" means there is no nightly score for that app. It is unknown, not zero.
